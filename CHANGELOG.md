# Changelog

## 0.1.1 - 2026-11-10
- The tile can now be resized in the editor: it starts in one cell, and below 2 x 2 cells it shows solar, grid,
  house and battery as a list. Drag its right edge, then its bottom edge, to give it the diagram.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-10
- First version: the energy flow tile with solar, grid, house, battery and up to four strings.
- A tap opens production and consumption per day for 7, 14 or 30 days, with self-sufficiency and self-consumption.
